## General Overview

Slumbering Feline is a single-player, one-shot game featuring a feline-themed story. Step into the shoes of Momo, who finds herself lost in a strange dream and trying to escape from her slumber.

Slumbering Feline is currently available on Windows & Linux via Itch.io and Steam.

### Story

![Slumbering Feline atmosphere](screenshot/atm.gif)

Waking up in an unfamiliar world, Momo is equipped with the mystical gear she’s always desired.

Momo will explore, battle enemies, discover weak points, find the key, and make a daring escape. Can Momo find her way out of this dreamscape?

### DLC: Slumbering Feline - Starter Pack

![Slumbering Feline Starter Pack](screenshot/starter-pack-gameplay.gif)

Slumbering Feline - Starter Pack is a paid convenience DLC that adds optional early-game bonus resources for players who want a smoother start.

The pack adds a **Stray Cat Toy Box** near the starting point in every new game (including Nightmare mode). Each box contains:

- **50x Magic Stone**
- **25x Star Stone**
- **1x Random Celestial Rune**

Starter Pack can be enabled or disabled from **Main Menu > DLC** before beginning a new game, so players can choose whether to use the bonus rewards.

### DLC: Slumbering Feline - Nightmare

![Slumbering Feline Nightmare](screenshot/nightmare-gameplay.gif)

Slumbering Feline - Nightmare is a paid challenge DLC for players who want a more intense version of the base game.

Nightmare mode upgrades enemy behavior with tougher stats, faster movement, quicker respawn, and deadlier attacks. Players must also manage the new **Nightmare Mark** effect, which gradually reduces Mental Health over time.

The DLC introduces exclusive progression options, including new Celestial Runes such as **Moonlight** (reveals nearby Nightmare Weakness) and **Nightmare Antibody** (immunity to Nightmare Mark), while also increasing Momo's rune slots from 3 to 4.

Nightmare is unlocked after clearing the base game once and owning the Nightmare DLC. It can then be selected from **New Game** on the main menu.

### Features

- **2D side-scrolling platformer** with pixel-art and an atmospheric environment.
- Unique combat system featuring **Shutter Attacks and Parry Actions**.
- **10-30** minutes playtime. (+25-40 minutes playtime on Nightmare)
- **A single objective** within a large, interconnected level.
- **7 enemy types, 2 weapons, plus a parry mechanic**. (+5 enemy variants on Nightmare)
- **15 Celestial Runes**; unique power-ups to discover and utilize. (+2 Nightmare rune on Nightmare)
- **4 checkpoints** for respawn and progression.
- Hidden quest, Side quest and various Puzzles.
- **Xbox controller** support (Windows).
- **37 Steam achievements**
- Available on **Windows** & **Linux**.
- English Language.
